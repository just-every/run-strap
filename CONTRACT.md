# Strap contract

Native owner sessions manage access and approve money or publication. Member sessions only book/cancel their own classes and acknowledge the current waiver; instructors check attendance for assigned classes. Revoked grants fail on the next request. Agent keys only prepare reviewable drafts.

D1 is authoritative for capacity, credits, payments and approvals. A booking transaction reserves a seat and spends one eligible credit together. Waitlisting spends none. Cancellation refunds once and promotes eligible FIFO waiters in the same transaction. External payment references are unique. Money records are receipts of externally received funds, never charges to a processor.

Public-door integration coverage is described in ACCEPTANCE.md and tests/strap.test.mjs. CLI archive coverage is in tests/archive.test.mjs. No hosted provider delivery, legal signature or real-export compatibility is claimed.
