# Strap product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Strap is the complete boutique fitness and yoga studio system a US owner buys to sell and auto-renew memberships, intro offers and class packs, collect payments, fill classes through a branded member app and follow people from first inquiry to sustained attendance. A yoga, Pilates or boutique fitness studio with 100–500 members can replace its incumbent without running billing, messaging, payroll preparation or retention in a second studio system.

This contract describes the destination, not a claim that it ships today. The status table records the distance from current code and tests; missing provider access, app distribution, payment migration or production evidence remains delivery work. USD, US buyers, US addresses/phone numbers, sales tax, ACH and W-2/1099 payroll handoff are the first-market defaults. Existing Australian copy and owner-approved homepage/UI remain untouched by this documentation change; STUDIO-001 is the required US product behavior.

## Reading and operating rules

Each named clause states buyer behavior and acceptance evidence through a screen, HTTP route, provider webhook, downloadable artifact or public command. ACCEPTANCE.md repeats every journey and its current status. Network/payment fakes belong only at the test edge; real provider delivery, store distribution and real-export compatibility need their own controlled evidence. Partial means a cited foundation exists while the named gap remains.

The previous contract had no clause IDs. Its positive guarantees receive published IDs below; no existing ID is renamed. Its receipt-only ceiling becomes one payment path alongside Stripe collection. Its disclaimer about unproved hosted delivery, legal signatures and real exports becomes an honest current-status gap rather than a limit on the target.

- Fresh installations start empty and owners enter or import real studio data.
- Monetary records use exact USD cents and explicit tax/rounding rules; entitlements and money remain separate ledgers.
- Every handler authorizes the current actor independently. Owners control staff rights, policy and automation; agents cannot self-approve refunds, debt waivers or contract changes.
- Approved recurring terms authorize scheduled renewal and routine notices under recorded policy. Changed facts invalidate stale previews and exceptional changes require a person's exact approval.
- Capacity, credit use, payment attempts, rewards and delivered events have stable identities so retries and restarts cannot create duplicate business effects. Pending and uncertain provider states remain visible.
- Import originals, financial corrections and audit histories remain attributable. Erasure and exports respect retention duties and do not pretend to erase external recipients or offline copies.

## Published guarantees

- **AUTH-001 — Native roles and private records.** Native human owners manage access and approve publication and money drafts. Members see their own balances, packs and bookings and book or cancel only their own classes; instructors see assigned rosters and check attendance only for assigned classes. Every page, JSON command and download checks its own authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in through invitations as an owner, two members and two instructors; read state, submit commands and request reminder downloads across roles; unauthorized work is refused without writes.

- **AUTH-002 — Immediate revocation and origin protection.** Deactivating a member or instructor or revoking an agent credential rejects its next request. Browser mutations require the configured same origin; bearer credentials cannot impersonate human sessions. **Acceptance evidence:** Deactivate an invited member and revoke an agent key, then reuse their sessions or credentials; attempt cross-origin and malformed commands; require refusal and unchanged records.

- **AGENT-001 — Exact drafts and human approval.** Agents can read studio context and prepare immutable class weeks, finite packs and balance-reminder drafts. A native human owner approves the exact draft once; changed source facts invalidate approval. Approval keeps the human identity and time. Reminder approval exports reviewed text. **Acceptance evidence:** Use an agent key to prepare a pack and reminder, refuse agent approval and the human endpoint with that key, approve as owner, download the text and refuse duplicate approval.

- **DATA-001 — Authoritative transactional studio records.** D1 is authoritative for people, capacity, credits, recorded payments and approvals. State revision and transaction fences prevent concurrent requests from overspending a credit or seat; waiting consumes no credit. **Acceptance evidence:** Race public booking commands for one seat and one credit; inspect one confirmed booking, no negative credit balance and no duplicate committed effect.

- **BOOK-001 — Eligible credit bookings.** A published future class has an assigned instructor and explicit capacity. A member books only with the current waiver acknowledged and an unexpired eligible finite credit; duplicate booking is refused and the seat and credit commit together. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish a reviewed future class, grant packs, book through the member API, attempt a duplicate and missing-waiver booking and inspect capacity and credit state.

- **BOOK-002 — Cancellation and eligible FIFO promotion.** Before class start an owner or the booked member cancels once, returns one spent credit and promotes the first eligible waiting member in the same transaction. Ineligible waiters cannot spend another member's credits. Configurable late-cancel rules extend this under ATT-002, and front-desk staff and managers cancel, move and restore a member's booking on the member's behalf under SCHED-014. **Acceptance evidence:** Fill a one-seat class, add a waiter, refuse another member's cancellation, cancel the booked seat and inspect one refunded credit and the waiter's confirmed seat and single debit.

- **PACK-001 — Reviewed finite class packs.** The owner or an agent prepares a member-specific finite pack with credits, amount and expiry. Human approval creates the pack, owed charge and retained approval once. Class credits remain distinct from money owed. Target USD presentation and processor collection extend this foundation. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a ten-credit pack, inspect no credits before approval, approve once and inspect ten credits, the exact owed cents and approval; repeat approval is refused.

- **PAY-001 — Once-only external receipt recording.** Owners record externally received funds against a member with positive whole cents and a unique external reference. Reusing the reference cannot reduce the balance twice. Processor collection is also required by BILL clauses; receipts are one supported payment path. **Acceptance evidence:** Record a partial external receipt against an approved pack, repeat its reference and submit a negative amount; inspect one payment and the remaining balance.

- **WAIVER-001 — Exact current waiver acknowledgment.** The owner publishes immutable waiver text versions. Members acknowledge the exact current version; publishing a new version requires renewed acknowledgment before booking. An acknowledgment is a version receipt; signed waiver execution is MEM-005. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish two waiver versions, reject booking without acknowledgment and acknowledgment of the obsolete version, acknowledge the latest and book successfully.

- **IMPORT-001 — Reviewed member CSV and reversible journal.** The owner previews the documented four-column Mindbody-shaped UTF-8 CSV, reviews its fingerprint and applies those exact bytes. Imports create inactive members with no invented credits or charges, retain originals and a resumable journal, and undo untouched imports while preserving later edits. Real incumbent export coverage is MOVE-001 through MOVE-006. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload the documented CSV through import.preview, refuse altered bytes with the old fingerprint, apply and resume to completion, inspect originals, edit one member and undo; the edited member survives.

- **ARCHIVE-001 — Complete native archive and guarded restore.** The owner exports and restores studio people, classes, packs, bookings, payments, approvals, waivers, import originals and journals using rer export/import. Damaged archives and populated destinations are refused. Sessions, sign-in links, agent keys, provider secrets and development outboxes stay installation-local. A later erasure journal fences an older archive; offline copies cannot discover later erasure unaided. **Acceptance evidence:** Populate a studio through HTTP, export with the public CLI, restore into an empty destination and re-export equal product parts; refuse corruption, a populated destination and a pre-erasure archive with a later journal.

- **ERASE-001 — Unused identity erasure fence.** The owner can erase an unused, uninvited, non-imported identity. Its identifying data is redacted and an identity fence prevents recreation, CSV reimport and resurrection through a restore supplied with the later journal. Used-member erasure is a further target under SEC-003. **Acceptance evidence:** Erase an unused person through person.erase, inspect absence of their email, refuse recreation and CSV reimport, and run the CLI older-backup/later-journal refusal.

- **AUDIT-001 — Retained draft approval records.** Class, pack and reminder approvals retain the approved draft, human approver and time. These records travel with native studio archives rather than being discarded on restore. Full staff and automation auditing is SEC-002. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a pack through HTTP, inspect its draft-linked approval, archive the populated studio and compare the restored approval records.

- **PUBLIC-001 — Run site and installed studio doors.** The run-site homepage opens /run and serves owner guides at /docs. An installed studio has a separate Lobby at /, setup and sign-in doors and private /app routes. These existing owner-approved pages keep their design and behavior. **Acceptance evidence:** Build the run site, open its homepage, /run and /docs; open the installed Worker Lobby signed out and attempt /app before and after sign-in. Confirm the public and private routes remain separate.

## Target clauses

### Studio and members

- **STUDIO-001 — US studio configuration.** Owners configure the legal studio identity, location addresses, USD pricing, US phone defaults, receipt details, local time zones, business hours and daylight-saving behavior; cents and local dates are explicit everywhere. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a New York studio and a Los Angeles location; buy a USD pack, view its receipt and book across a DST transition; local times and charged cents agree across staff and member views.

- **STUDIO-002 — Locations and shared memberships.** One business manages multiple studios with location-scoped staff, rooms, schedules, prices and reporting; membership eligibility explicitly permits selected or all locations and transfer does not duplicate credits. **Acceptance evidence:** Give a member a two-location plan, book at both, deny a third location, and compare site-restricted manager and consolidated owner reports.

- **MEM-001 — Unified member history.** Staff with permission open one member profile showing contact details, preferences, emergency contact, notes, membership state, pack usage, bookings, attendance, communications, waivers and financial history, with duplicate-merge review preserving source identities. Staff holding the member-record right (STAFF-001) create a member or walk-in profile at the desk or by phone and send the account invitation, and edit contact details and emergency contact on the member's behalf; each change records the staff member, channel and before and after values (SEC-002). **Acceptance evidence:** Open a migrated member, book, attend, pay and reply; see the dated timeline and ledger, merge a duplicate with review and confirm all histories remain linked. As front desk, create a phoned-in member's profile, send the invitation and correct their phone number; each change appears in the timeline and the audit as staff-made, and a front-desk user without the member-record right is refused with no writes.

- **MEM-002 — Households and authorized payers.** Households retain individual participants, guardians, permissioned contacts and a shared payer; parents book and sign for authorized minors without exposing unrelated household records or granting the payer blanket staff access. Staff holding the member-record right set up a household, link a guardian and minor and change permissioned contacts on the members' behalf; making a person the payer completes only after that payer's own payment-method authorization (BILL-004) on the studio device or by secure link. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a household with two children and a payer, book each child and charge their payer, remove a guardian and verify the next request loses child access. Front desk adds a third child at the desk, and a change of payer to the second guardian completes only after that guardian saves a method on the studio device; both changes are audited as staff-made.

- **MEM-003 — Family pricing and tuition.** Owners set sibling or household discounts and recurring tuition per participant with transparent stacking rules, payer allocation and end dates; removing one child recalculates future dues without rewriting past charges. **Acceptance evidence:** Enroll two siblings, preview and collect discounted monthly tuition, withdraw one and verify the next bill and participant entitlements.

- **MEM-004 — Intake forms and restricted notes.** Studios build required and optional intake forms for signup and selected services, version questions and restrict sensitive answers by role; a changed required form prompts completion before the relevant enrollment. Staff holding the member-record right enter answers a member gives by phone, marked as staff-entered with that provenance, or launch the form on the desk tablet in locked signing mode (MEM-005); entering or reading restricted answers also needs the sensitive-notes right. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish an intake form, complete it on signup, change a required question and attempt a new enrollment; front desk sees permitted answers while unauthorized instructors cannot read restricted notes. Front desk enters a phoned-in member's answers; they show as staff-entered in the profile and audit, and an instructor still cannot read the restricted answer.

- **MEM-005 — Signed waivers and enrollment policies.** Signup and enrollment collect current waiver and service-specific policy signatures, guardian authority where needed, exact document version, signer attribution and completion evidence. Members receive a downloadable signed copy; expired or missing waivers block the affected service. At the desk, staff hand a tablet to a walk-in or guest in a locked signing mode that shows only that person's documents, records the device and launching staff member, and returns to a lock screen without exposing other records. The same mode presents a staff-sold membership's agreement and recurring-payment authorization for the member to review, accept and sign (BILL-014), and the signed agreement is stored on the member's profile. Staff holding the check-in right launch signing for any member at the desk or resend the member's signing link from the profile; staff never sign or acknowledge for the member. MOVE-011 obligation before this capability ships: its studio records and documents must join the portable exit with stable relationships and provenance; a native restore keeps its studio billing and delivery disconnected until the human owner reviews and approves reconnection. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign a waiver on mobile as an adult and authorized guardian, download each evidence package, replace the policy and verify re-signing is required before the affected booking; at the desk launch tablet signing for a walk-in, sign and check in, and verify the tablet returns locked with no access to other members' records; resend a member's signing link from their profile and see the launch audited as staff-made while the signature records only the member.

- **MEM-006 — Member preferences and privacy choices.** Members control contact channels, marketing consent, preferred times and classes, accessibility requests and data-sharing choices. Staff holding the member-record right change these preferences on the member's behalf with recorded provenance: an opt-out takes effect immediately, while a marketing opt-in records the member's stated consent, channel and source, and an SMS marketing opt-in is confirmed by the member's own reply (MSG-005) before any marketing text. Marketing permission is independent of a booking or transactional notice. **Acceptance evidence:** Save different transactional and marketing preferences, book a class, attempt a marketing sequence and export the preference record with its source and date. Front desk records a phoned-in SMS opt-out that suppresses the next marketing text, and a verbal SMS opt-in that sends no marketing text until the member confirms by reply; both are audited as staff-made.

### Plans and memberships

- **PLAN-001 — Recurring memberships and product catalog.** Owners sell auto-renewing monthly, annual and fixed-term memberships, finite class packs, drop-ins and intro offers from a versioned catalog with USD prices, commitment terms and eligible services, through member checkout (BILL-002) or a staff sale at the desk or on a staff device (BILL-014); members see the complete price and renewal terms before purchase. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish one of each product, purchase through member checkout, sell the monthly membership to another member at the desk under BILL-014, then change the catalog price; existing contract terms and purchase evidence remain intact for both purchases.

- **PLAN-002 — Unlimited and period entitlements.** Memberships grant unlimited access or credits per week/month with per-service and location limits, time-of-day and weekday windows (such as off-peak memberships), rollover rules, concurrent-booking caps and the guest-pass allowance of PLAN-010. The entitlement ledger distinguishes reservation, attendance, cancellation and expiry. **Acceptance evidence:** Buy a four-visit monthly plan and an unlimited plan, cross a period boundary, book eligible and ineligible classes concurrently and inspect exactly the allowed reservations; an off-peak plan books a 2:00 pm class and is refused a 6:00 pm class.

- **PLAN-003 — Pack validity and credit history.** Members and staff see remaining, reserved, consumed, restored and expired pack credits with eligible services and exact expiration in the studio time zone. Staff adjustments require a reason and preserve history. Until STAFF-001 adds staff rights, the human owner makes adjustments. Service names match ignoring case and extra whitespace; an empty eligible-services list permits all classes. A class uses its explicit service or, when blank, its class name (older room-encoded titles use the class-name portion). A confirmed booking reserves one available credit, check-in marks that reservation consumed without a second debit, and cancellation returns it once. Reserved and consumed counts describe current allocations; restored is cumulative. Expiration is exclusive at the stored instant and evaluated against both booking time and class start; a browser Good through date includes that whole local day and expires at the following local midnight. Owner adjustments change available credits and/or expiration, with an exact reason, pack revision and pack-scoped retry identity; original purchase credits, price, charge and earlier events remain unchanged. Changing expiration affects unused credits; existing reservations keep their allocated credits. Every new grant, reservation, consumption, restoration and adjustment retains its actor and time. Older packs retain recorded balances and booking facts in an explicitly labelled legacy opening balance; unknown earlier events are labelled unavailable, never invented. Future plan and booking doors PLAN-001, PLAN-002, PLAN-004–011, SCHED-002, SCHED-005, SCHED-010–014 and BILL-002/014 inherit the same eligibility and credit-history obligations before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Buy a pack restricted to mat classes, attempt a reformer booking, consume and return a credit, cross expiry and inspect the immutable usage and adjustment history. Exercise a reasoned adjustment and its replay, refuse a changed or stale request and an unauthorized member or instructor adjustment, and confirm member and owner views agree while another member cannot read the history.

- **PLAN-004 — Intro offers and eligibility.** Owners configure intro duration, included visits, new-client or returning-client eligibility, one-per-person/household limits and explicit conversion choices. Intro expiry cannot silently enroll a member into unaccepted renewal terms. Staff holding the sale right sell an intro offer at the desk or by phone under the same eligibility checks; selling outside eligibility needs the price right and a reason, audited (SEC-002). **Acceptance evidence:** Purchase a two-week intro as a new member, refuse a duplicate purchase under another account linked to the same member, use its visits and accept or decline an explicit recurring offer. Front desk is refused a second intro for the same person, and a manager's eligibility exception with a reason is sold once and audited.

- **PLAN-005 — Upgrades downgrades and proration preview.** Members request or make policy-permitted plan changes, or staff holding the contract-change right (STAFF-001) make them on a member's behalf from the profile, with effective date, remaining credits, prorated charge or credit and next renewal shown before confirmation. Staff approve exceptions and changed source facts invalidate a stale preview. **Acceptance evidence:** Upgrade mid-cycle, accept the preview, downgrade at renewal and race a second change; charge and entitlements match the confirmed preview and stale approval is refused.

- **PLAN-006 — Freezes and planned resumption.** Members request a freeze with dates and reason in self-service, and authorized staff approve exceptions to a member's request; staff holding the contract-change right also freeze a membership directly on the member's behalf under PLAN-011, with no member request to approve. Freeze fees, billing-date movement, visit access and booked classes are previewed, notices are delivered and the plan resumes once on its agreed date. **Acceptance evidence:** Freeze a plan with future bookings, inspect the exact billing and class effects, approve, cross the dates and verify one resumption and the revised renewal charge; a staff-made freeze under PLAN-011 shows the same preview and effects.

- **PLAN-007 — Cancellation and remaining value.** Members can cancel auto-renewal or submit a term-bound request in self-service, and staff holding the contract-change right terminate a membership on the member's behalf under PLAN-011 (for an emailed, phoned-in or at-the-desk cancellation); confirmation states effective date, final charge, refund policy, remaining credits and future booking disposition. Cancellation stops future renewals and reconciles every surviving entitlement. **Acceptance evidence:** Cancel a plan with unused credits and future reservations, approve a required exception, verify confirmation and final bill, then advance beyond renewal and confirm no further charge; a staff termination of a second plan under PLAN-011 shows the same confirmation and final bill.

- **PLAN-008 — Renewal disclosures and price changes.** The studio delivers scheduled renewal, expiring-pack and price-change notices under the member's accepted terms, preserves the notice and acceptance basis, and offers the applicable cancellation or plan-change path. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule an annual renewal and a price increase, deliver the notices at the configured lead time, cancel from the notice and verify no renewal collection.

- **PLAN-009 — Courses workshops and tuition installments.** Owners sell a complete course, workshop or semester with enrollment caps, deposit, installment schedule and per-session attendance; withdrawal and missed installment rules show their financial and seat effects before approval. Staff holding the sale right enroll a participant at the desk or by phone, and staff holding the contract-change right withdraw one on the member's behalf with the same effects previewed. **Acceptance evidence:** Enroll a household participant in a six-week course, collect the deposit and installments, record attendance and withdraw under policy; roster, installments and credited balance reconcile. Front desk enrolls a second participant by phone and later withdraws them under the same preview; both actions are audited as staff-made.

- **PLAN-010 — Guest passes and guest allowances.** Memberships and packs can include guest passes per period (for example two a month) with eligible services, expiry, rollover and a per-guest limit such as one complimentary visit per guest per year. The studio chooses what happens when a member exceeds the allowance: refuse the booking or charge the guest drop-in price to the member's payer. Guest-pass use sits in the entitlement ledger separately from the member's own credits, and a canceled guest seat restores the pass once. **Acceptance evidence:** Give a member a plan with two monthly guest passes, use both, attempt a third guest booking in the same period under each policy and see it refused or charged once to the payer; cross the period boundary and verify the allowance resets; another member cannot book the same guest on a guest pass beyond the per-guest limit.

- **PLAN-011 — Staff-processed freezes and terminations.** Staff holding the contract-change right (STAFF-001: held by owners and managers, grantable to front desk) freeze or terminate a member's membership on the member's behalf from the member profile in the staff web app or on a staff phone or tablet (APP-004), without any member request in self-service. A freeze takes a start date and an end date or no end date (open-ended until staff end it), and a studio-defined freeze type (for example Medical or Travel) that pre-fills its reason, freeze fee, default length and allowed start days, which staff may adjust within policy. A termination takes a termination date (immediately, at the end of the current billing period, or at the end of the plan's notice period or commitment term), a studio-defined termination reason code (for example Moved away or Price) and a comment. Before confirming, staff see the same preview as PLAN-006 and PLAN-007: billing-date movement or the final charge, any freeze or early-termination fee, which scheduled autopays in BILL-013 are moved after the freeze or removed by the termination, remaining credits and the future bookings affected and how they are handled under policy. On confirmation the BILL-013 schedule matches the preview exactly, autopays of a frozen membership show as scheduled after the freeze, the member receives the usual confirmation notice (MSG-001), and the action is recorded as staff-made with actor, type or reason code and comment (SEC-002). On the termination date access ends, exactly one final charge is collected where due, no later renewal is created, and future bookings past the date are canceled with credits handled under the cancellation policy. A scheduled termination can be canceled until its effective date, which restores the removed future autopays exactly once with no period charged twice or dropped; staff can end a freeze early, and BILL-005 then resumes billing once from the revised date. Freeze types and termination reason codes are managed by owners; termination reason codes feed churn by reason in RPT-003 and scheduled terminations appear in RPT-007. Agents may prepare a freeze or termination as a draft for a human to confirm but cannot execute one. **Acceptance evidence:** As front desk holding the contract-change right on a 390-pixel staff view, terminate a monthly member at the end of a 30-day notice period with reason code "Moved away"; the preview lists the one remaining charge and the removed later autopays, and the BILL-013 schedule then matches it. Advance past the date: exactly one final charge, no later renewal, access ends on the date, and future bookings past it are canceled with credits handled under policy. On a second member, schedule a termination and then cancel it before the date; the original autopay schedule returns with no period charged twice or dropped. Freeze a third member for 30 days using a "Medical" freeze type with a $10 fee; the fee is charged once, the renewal moves by 30 days, and ending the freeze early on day 20 resumes billing once from the revised date. RPT-003 churn by reason shows "Moved away". A front-desk user without the right is refused with no writes, an agent key is refused, and all actions appear in the SEC-002 audit.

### Scheduling and attendance

- **SCHED-001 — Recurring timetable and series edits.** Owners publish repeating schedules with local times, instructor, location, room and capacity; edit one occurrence or this-and-future occurrences with a preview of existing bookings and notifications. DST keeps the intended local hour. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a weekly class across DST, move one session and then the remaining series, inspect affected bookings and confirm members receive only the relevant changes.

- **SCHED-002 — Private appointments and services.** Members book private sessions from service duration, staff availability, location, equipment and buffers; deposits, packages, rescheduling and cancellation use the same entitlement and billing rules as classes. Staff move or cancel a member's appointment on the member's behalf under SCHED-014. **Acceptance evidence:** Offer two overlapping appointment slots using one instructor and room, book concurrently, collect a deposit and reschedule; only one conflicting reservation commits.

- **SCHED-003 — Room equipment and instructor conflicts.** Scheduling blocks overlapping instructor, room, reformer or shared-equipment allocations, including preparation buffers, availability exceptions and concurrent edits; managers see the conflicting records before publication. Until STAFF-001 adds managers, the human owner acts for the manager on these doors. Room and equipment names identify resources across the current studio, ignoring case and leading, trailing or repeated whitespace; equipment includes individually named reformers. Preparation and cleanup are whole minutes from 0 through 1440 inclusive before and after each class (default zero), applied to every allocated resource. Conflict checks compare half-open UTC intervals `[start − preparation, end + cleanup)`, so exact boundary contact is allowed; the studio time zone controls entry and display. The owner records, updates or removes instructor, room and equipment unavailable intervals with a reason; an exception cannot overlap an already-published allocation. Only the human owner reads exception reasons; preparation agents receive the blocking resource, interval and record ID without the reason, and members and instructors receive no exception records. Preparation and publication both recheck current records under the shared transaction fence; a blocked draft names the conflicting record, resource and occupied interval, and no part of the draft publishes. The current class-week doors conform now; future scheduling doors SCHED-001, SCHED-002, SCHED-004, SCHED-005, SCHED-007, SCHED-008, SCHED-010–014 and CHANNEL-001–003 inherit these checks before they ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Try to publish overlapping classes using the same instructor, room or equipment, including a concurrent change; each conflict is refused and displayed without partial publication. Exercise both preparation buffers, unavailable intervals and exact touching boundaries through the public commands and browser forms; a new exception blocks a prepared draft but cannot invalidate a published class. Verify owner-only reasons are absent from member, instructor and agent responses, including agent preparation errors.

- **SCHED-004 — Instructor substitutions.** Instructors request coverage; managers see qualified available substitutes, approve one and update the assigned roster, pay rate and member notifications. Until approved the original assignment remains visible. **Acceptance evidence:** Request a substitute, reject a conflicting candidate, approve an available instructor and verify their roster rights and pay report change while the original instructor loses attendance rights.

- **SCHED-005 — Recurring member reservations.** Members reserve a recurring class or appointment slot within their entitlement and booking horizon, with explicit exceptions for full, closed or ineligible sessions; each occurrence has its own cancellation and waitlist outcome. Staff-created standing series, such as weekly privates, and recurring class reservations staff make for a member are SCHED-013. **Acceptance evidence:** Reserve a weekly class with one full occurrence and one holiday closure, inspect booked/waitlisted/skipped dates, cancel one and then the future series without double-credit effects; reserve a weekly private appointment from the member app with the same per-occurrence outcomes.

- **SCHED-006 — Booking windows and cancellation rules.** Owners configure opening and closing windows, member priority, cutoff times and cancellation policies by service or plan. Online-bookable capacity is set separately from total capacity under SCHED-011, and permissioned staff overrides of windows are SCHED-010. Members see the applicable rule before booking and the exact charge or credit effect before cancellation. Staff canceling or moving a booking for a member see the same effect before confirming and choose a late cancel under policy or an early cancel that waives it, under SCHED-014. **Acceptance evidence:** Book at both sides of a booking-window boundary and cancel before and after a service cutoff; the displayed policy, credit return and fee match persisted outcomes. As front desk, cancel a member's booking after the cutoff and see the same fee in the staff preview before choosing late or early cancel. Semantics: a service's rule replaces the studio rule, and with neither booking stays open until start with every pre-start cancellation early. Hours and minutes are elapsed time before the class's UTC start (a DST change moves the wall-clock boundary); booking is allowed from the opening instant inclusive until the closing instant exclusive, checked again at commit on the database clock; a cancellation at or after the cutoff instant is late. Member priority is a pack sold with priority booking: inside the priority-only window only such a pack can seat the member. Each booking keeps the cancellation terms in force when it was made (cutoff, credit effect, fee), so a later rule change never worsens it; bookings made before SCHED-006 keep the original free-cancellation rule. A late cancel either keeps the spent credit (recorded as consumed) or returns it, and adds the fee to the member's balance as one charge; Strap shows that as a fee added, never as a card charge or a collected payment, which ATT-002 and BILL-004 carry. Leaving a waitlist is always free. The shown effect carries a terms key; a cancel sent with a key that no longer matches is refused with no writes. Until STAFF-001 the human owner acts for front desk and holds the fee-waiver right: after the cutoff the owner must choose a late cancel or an early cancel that returns the credit and assesses no fee, recording a required reason; members cannot waive and instructors and agents cannot cancel for members. Windows apply to owner desk bookings too; the audited override is SCHED-010's. Future doors inherit these rules before they ship: SCHED-010 (window override), SCHED-014 (moves, staff reasons, notices, cancellations list), ATT-002 (fee authorization, pending versus collected, collection), PLAN-001/PLAN-002 (plan-level rules and priority for memberships), MSG-004 (late-window promotion stop), and SCHED-002, SCHED-005, SCHED-007, SCHED-009, SCHED-012 and SCHED-013 for their bookings.

- **SCHED-007 — Spot and reformer selection.** Members select a named room spot, bike or reformer from an accessible map or list; disabled spots are unavailable, instructors see assignments and concurrent requests cannot claim the same equipment.
