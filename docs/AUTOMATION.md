# Private automation review

The owner configures **Private automation settings** in Studio. These rules prepare private work for the owner; they never send a member message, collect money or grant a reward. Existing intro and renewal message reviews remain separate, explicit actions.

First approve an intro sequence in Lead follow-up or a renewal sequence in the Renewal worklist. In Studio, choose that retained sequence and its channel, set the UTC start and end, evaluation interval and lifetime private-task budget, and preview the current audience. Renewal eligibility uses the chosen horizon and low-credit threshold. The preview names every retained source and explains any current exclusion. Changed source or preference facts require another preview before enabling.

Scheduled evaluation creates at most one private task for each retained intro purchase or native renewal pack, even across retries, runtime restarts and replacement rules. The lifetime task budget includes retained and suppressed tasks; pausing or resuming cannot replenish it. Runs retain their queued, skipped and exception outcomes, including budget exhaustion. Lead follow-up shows intro history; the Renewal worklist shows renewal history.

Pause a rule in Studio to hold its queued work. Resume by previewing the current audience and explicitly confirming it. Every evaluation and private-task completion checks current permission, member activity, source eligibility, sequence state and the schedule. Renewal or expiry stops an ineligible source. An intro stops after native membership purchase or a member reply. Withdrawn consent permanently suppresses existing work; renewed permission does not revive its trigger. Completing private review records who reviewed it and sends nothing.

Native archives keep rules, private tasks and history. Execution connections and confirmation authority do not travel. After restoring, Studio shows **Execution disconnected**. Previously captured confirmation cannot reconnect it. The owner must preview current eligibility and explicitly confirm resumption. Durable source identities still prevent duplicate work. Verified erasure preserves only suppressed trigger chronology without the erased member's personal values.

Automatic member-message dispatch, charges, rewards, financial budgets and provider execution remain unavailable. Email and SMS permission are separate from delivery readiness. These controls do not establish live SMS, Postmark, Mailchimp or push delivery, household authority or recurring-payment permission.
