## ATT-001

Only the human owner pairs a kiosk, from a signed-in owner session: pairing makes a one-time link valid 15 minutes whose secret is stored hashed; confirming it on the device mints a 32-byte random device token, stored hashed and held as an HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie scoped to `/kiosk`; the owner names and revokes kiosks, and a revoked kiosk is refused on its next request and at commit.

## ATT-001

A kiosk is its own role with no session: it cannot open `/app`, the state or any command API, and agents can never pair or act as a kiosk.

## APP-003

Members use responsive web and native views to manage their own profile, household rights, bookings, waitlist positions, invoices, credits, payment methods and policy-permitted renewal, cancellation and freeze requests. done covers every member self-service capability that exists today. A member's profile is their name, sign-in email, 10-digit US mobile (the ATT-001 kiosk phone) and one emergency contact (name, 10-digit US phone and an optional relationship of up to 60 characters). The signed-in member edits their own name, mobile and emergency contact on the My profile page at 390 pixels and desktop (`profile.save`); the sign-in email is shown but stays an owner-only identity change (`person.save`), and a member request naming an email or another person is refused. The member-record staff door (`profile.save.staff`; the native owner until STAFF-001) edits the same fields with the same validation and records the desk, phone or inbox channel, and the owner's identity door (`person.save`) also records a member's name, email or mobile change. Every change writes exactly one profile change entry with the actor, role, channel, changed fields and exact before and after values; a staff-made entry is the canonical STAFF-006 audit with the member-record right. Absent fields keep their value; an emergency contact needs a name and a phone, and clearing both (with the relationship) removes it. A matching request identity returns the recorded profile and entry; a changed request under the same identity, a stale profile revision, an invalid phone, a half contact, an unchanged profile, another person's profile or an unauthorized actor is refused with no writes. Profile edits send no message. The owner and the member read the same current profile and change history; other members, instructors and the kiosk read none of it, and preparation-only agent keys read it only as the owner's studio context (AGENT-001). The emergency contact is a third party's personal data: the current contact and every change entry are retained while the member's record is, travel with the member in the native archive and the portable exit (MOVE-011), restore only with the member, and go with the member's erasure, which deletes the contact and keeps each change entry's actor, role, right, channel, time and changed field names without their values; a pre-erasure archive cannot restore them over a later erasure journal (ARCHIVE-001). Bookings, waitlist positions and offers, credits and balances keep their existing member doors (BOOK-001, BOOK-002, SCHED-006, SCHED-011, MSG-004, PLAN-003, PAY-001). Each future capability inherits this obligation with its own before-shipping acceptance: BILL-004 (card replacement and stored payment methods), PLAN-006 (freeze requests), MEM-002 (household rights), PLAN-007 (cancellation requests), PLAN-008 (renewal requests), BILL-009 (invoices), APP-001 (native views) and SEC-003 (used-member erasure of the contact and its change values). MOVE-011 obligation before this capability ships: its studio records and documents must join the portable exit with stable relationships and provenance; a native restore keeps its studio billing and delivery disconnected until the human owner reviews and approves reconnection.

## API-002

Owners create least-privilege, location-aware application credentials, rotate and revoke them, set permitted event subscriptions and inspect usage and failures. Pagination, schema versions and documented rate responses make integrations operable without exposing full owner data. this clause's target is exactly the catalogue below and the lifecycle around it; further read scopes (such as contact details or money) and any write scope each need their own owner-approved clause and acceptance before they join the catalogue. Only the native human owner's session manages integration keys, and secrets are issued only in the app: the Integrations page form creates a key and rotates its secret (`integration.create`, `integration.rotate`), the JSON command door refuses both even for the owner (403 `integration_secret_in_app_only`), and the owner revokes a key or changes its events on the page or through the owner command door (`integration.revoke`, `integration.events`); preparation-only agent keys, integration keys, members, instructors and kiosks are refused with no writes. A key's secret is 256 random bits shown once; Strap stores only its SHA-256 hash, as for preparation-only agent keys, and a key works only while the owner who created it keeps the same active owner access. The fixed read-only catalogue holds `roster:read` and the separately granted `attendance:read`. The `roster:read` scope returns classes (id, title, service, UTC start and end, capacity, published or cancelled) with each booking's id, status (booked, waitlisted, offered or cancelled) and the attendee's first name plus last initial; it never carries attendance, money, packs, payments, fees, balances, contact details, notes, cancellation reasons or instructor names. The separate GET resource `/api/strap/integration/attendance` requires `attendance:read`, which is off by default and granted explicitly by the owner only when creating a new key on Integrations. Scopes stay fixed for a key's life: no existing key gains attendance and rotation never widens scopes. For a key created before API-003 shipped it uses the roster's class paging and returns only each class id and each currently booked booking's `bookingId` and `attended` boolean. True means its current outcome is attended, by check-in or owner correction; false covers unrecorded, no-show and excused without distinguishing them. Waitlisted, offered and cancelled bookings are omitted. No names, outcome labels, times, actors, channels, kiosk ids, fees, credits, reasons, corrections or notes appear; a key also granted `roster:read` may join on bookingId. Naming no-show, excused or cancellation outcomes, or a member reference, is API-003, which applies only to keys created after it shipped. Every API-002 rule applies to both scopes, including the owner-only lifecycle, check order, shared per-key rate limit, logs, rotation, revocation, location, erasure and restore fences. Every key covers the studio's one location (the Studio settings location) until STUDIO-002 adds location scopes. The integration door (`/api/strap/integration/`) answers GET only: any other method is refused 405 `read_only`, and any resource outside the key's scopes, including payments, balances, packs and people, 403 `scope_not_granted`, with no writes. Every request names `strap-schema-version: 1`; a missing or unknown version is refused 400 with the supported versions. Roster pages hold 1–100 classes (default 50) in UTC start-then-id order after an opaque cursor; an invalid limit or cursor is refused, never clamped. Each key may make 60 counted requests in any rolling 60 seconds: every request with a live secret counts, whatever its outcome, except those refused by the limit, which are answered 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After` in whole seconds (1–60) until the oldest counted request leaves the window. Strap checks the secret, then the limit, the method, the schema version and the scope. Rotation commits the new secret and the old secret's revocation together; revocation stops every secret of the key at once, and a revoked key cannot be rotated or resubscribed. The owner who created a key reads its usage and failures: the newest 100 requests per key with time, method, path, status and refusal code, never a query string, member data or secret; a request with an unknown secret cannot be attributed and is not logged. A key subscribes only to events its scopes permit (`roster:read`: `class.changed` and `booking.changed`; `attendance:read`: `attendance.changed`); Strap records the subscriptions and labels them not delivered until INT-008 delivers signed events. Keys, secrets, subscriptions, logs and rate counts never travel in native archives or the portable exit, so a restored studio has no integration keys and refuses every old secret (ARCHIVE-001, MOVE-011); erased people never appear in either scope. Named carriers inherit these rules with their own before-shipping acceptance: STUDIO-002 (location scopes), INT-008 (signed delivery of subscribed, scope-permitted events) and SEC-003 (used-member erasure leaves both scopes).

## API-003

Owners let another application, such as a front-desk display or a studio dashboard, read who booked, attended, missed or cancelled each class through a separate explicit `attendance:read` scope on a new integration key, with a stable per-key member reference and short names, and without reasons, notes, health answers, money or contact details. `attendance:read` is a separate explicit scope that the owner grants only when creating a new key on Integrations, where the form lists the exact shared fields before the key exists. Scopes stay fixed for a key's life: no existing key gains attendance, rotation never widens scopes, and `roster:read` stays attendance-free. Every API-002 rule applies unchanged: owner-only lifecycle with secrets shown only in the app and stored as SHA-256 hashes, GET only, `strap-schema-version: 1`, pages of 1–100 classes (default 50) in UTC start-then-id order, 60 counted requests per key in any rolling 60 seconds shared with every resource, the owner-only usage log, immediate rotation and revocation, exclusion from native archives and the portable exit, and the studio's one location until STUDIO-002 adds location scopes. GET `/api/strap/integration/attendance` returns, for each class on the page, its id, title, service, UTC start and end and status (published or cancelled), and for each seat with an outcome a row of exactly: `bookingId`, `memberRef`, `attendee`, `outcome`, `checkedInAt`, `checkInChannel`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy`, `corrected`, `correctedFrom`, `correctedAt` and `source`. `outcome` is one of `booked` (a confirmed seat nobody has checked in or marked), `attended`, `no_show`, `excused`, `late_cancel` and `early_cancel`; the outcome is the booking's current state, and a late arrival is not a state, so a consumer compares `checkedInAt` with the class start. A waitlisted or offered place has no outcome and is omitted, as is a cancellation that was not a late or early cancel choice (a move, an expired offer, a waitlist release, or the studio cancelling the class, which the class status shows). `checkedInAt` and `checkInChannel` are set only for a real check-in (roster or kiosk) and are null when the owner corrected the seat to attended. `cancelledAt` and `cancelledBy` (`member`, `staff` or `system`) are set only on a cancelled seat. `corrected` is true when the owner corrected the outcome, `correctedFrom` is the outcome before the latest correction (`no_show` or `late_cancel`) and `correctedAt` its UTC time, never the reason. `source` is `native`, or `imported` for a row that MOVE-009 brings in. `attendee` is the first name plus last initial, as in the roster. `memberRef` is `mref_` plus the first 128 bits, in hex, of HMAC-SHA-256 over the person id under a random secret held only by that key: it is the same for one member in every class and across secret rotation, differs between members, and cannot be joined across two keys or to a person id. The resource never carries correction, cancellation or waiver reasons, notes, staff alerts, waiver state, intake or health answers, contact details, kiosk ids, check-in codes, full names, person ids, money, fees, credits, packs, balances, instructor names or pay, or an erased person. A key created with `attendance:read` under API-002 alone, before this clause shipped, keeps its boolean-only resource and gains no field. A key subscribed to `attendance.changed` is told it is recorded and labelled not delivered until INT-008 delivers signed events. Carriers inherit these rules with their own before-shipping acceptance: INT-008 (the event carries only these fields), SEC-003 (used and imported member erasure removes the rows and the reference), STUDIO-002 (location scopes), MOVE-009 (imported rows and their source) and LEAD-006, MEM-001 and MEM-006 (a member sharing opt-out, if one is added, removes that member's rows). The owner’s explicit grant on a new key remains required. MEM-006 now also lets a member remove their rows from existing integration keys; it never widens a key’s scope.

## API-003

A new key created on Integrations with `attendance:read` (the form lists the fields; a roster-only key is refused the resource with 403 and its roster, even after rotation or crafted scope fields, carries no attendance) reads classes and rows with the exact field allowlist; asserts booked, attended (check-in), no_show, excused (owner correction), late_cancel and early_cancel with `cancelledBy`, `corrected`, `correctedFrom` and `correctedAt` (a late cancel corrected to early reads early_cancel, corrected from late_cancel), no check-in time for a corrected attendance, waitlisted, offered and studio-cancelled seats omitted, `source` imported once a row is marked, `memberRef` equal for one member across classes and rotation and different between members and between two keys, full name, email, phone, ids of people, reasons, money and instructor absent, an erased person absent, paging, the shared rate limit and usage log, `attendance.changed` recorded not delivered, and a key created under API-002 alone still reading booleans only.

## MOVE-009

## SEC-004

Owners enroll a second factor (passkey or TOTP authenticator) before reaching refunds, payouts, pay, exports, staff rights or provider connections. Recovery codes are single use, a factor reset requires another owner and is audited, and members may opt in. Strap's own factors protect Base sign-in. **Doors:** a person who must have a factor and has none is refused every gated page, form, JSON and download with 403 `mfa_enrollment_required`; ordinary work stays open. Anyone with a working factor (required or opted in) is held at every gated door with `mfa_required` until the session presents one, lasting that session. Gated today: revenue and teaching-pay reports; every CSV, XLSX, text and exit download; staff history; pay rules and every pay view other than an instructor's own; staff rights (inviting, deactivating, reactivating, changing a role, and the owner's finance-report grant); preparation-only agent keys; integration keys; kiosk pairing; the member-email connection; factor resets. Refunds (BILL-008), payouts (BILL-011), Stripe (BILL-001), payroll and provider connections (STAFF-003–005, INT-001–009, MSG and CHANNEL connections) join before they ship. Agent, integration and kiosk keys never satisfy, perform or bypass a factor check. **Factors:** TOTP RFC 6238 (SHA-1, six digits, 30 seconds, one step either side, each step once per factor), secret sealed AES-256-GCM under a dedicated `MFA_KEY` generated at setup (never the session secret), setup refused while `MFA_KEY` is absent, factor active after one valid code. Passkeys use WebAuthn with user verification required, the installation host as RP, ES256 or RS256, single-use challenges expiring after five minutes, and any attestation accepted without claiming the maker checked. **Enroll/remove:** a first factor needs Base fresh email-link step-up; adding or removing beside a working factor needs the session's check; a person who must have a factor cannot remove their last. **Recovery:** ten codes shown once, hashed, each once in one transaction, counting only while a working factor exists; regenerating after a factor check voids the old set. Five wrong since the last right code pauses that person's checks for 15 minutes. **Reset:** only another owner, in a factor-checked session, after Base step-up and with a required reason; removes factors and recovery codes and ends all their sessions. Nobody resets their own; a sole owner recovers only with a recovery code, with no support or installation bypass. **Audit:** SEC-002 staff history records every enrollment, removal, reset (actor, subject, time, reason, factors removed), recovery-code use, regeneration and pause; never a secret, code or credential. **Archives:** factor secrets, passkeys and recovery hashes never enter a native archive or MOVE-011 exit; a restored studio sends everyone to enrollment, and a changed `MFA_KEY` does the same for authenticator apps with the reason shown. **Finance-report grant:** gives a member read of the revenue and collections report and its downloads only; it is not STAFF-001's finance role, which replaces it when STAFF-001 ships. STAFF-001's manager and finance roles reach gated doors only through SEC-005, and no right grants reset power. SEC-001, SEC-002, SEC-003, MOVE-011, API-002, API-003 and APP-001 inherit these protections before their new doors ship.

## SCHED-008 mail configuration

Email goes through the installation's configured Base mail provider (MAIL_PROVIDER resend, sendgrid or cloudflare with its key, from MAIL_FROM), built apart from the sign-in sender, only after the cancellation commits, with the reservation id as the provider idempotency key; each reservation is claimed once and never resent.
