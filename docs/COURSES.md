# Courses and tuition

Open **Courses** to publish a workshop, course or semester with a fixed list of sessions, a seat cap, a deposit and dated installments. Session lines use an ISO start and end separated by ` | `. Installment lines use an ISO due time and a USD amount separated by ` | `. The published schedule and policy stay unchanged for accepted enrollments. The course name identifies its service for existing signing and intake policies; current required completion is checked at enrollment, and current waiver completion again at attendance.

Choose a participant and **Preview enrollment**. For a household participant, the currently accepted household payer receives the personal tuition review after signing in to Courses. Staff can open that review but cannot accept it. Once the payer accepts the exact price and installment schedule, staff selects **Confirm enrollment**; available capacity is checked again. Acceptance alone reserves no seat and charges no balance. Confirmation creates one tuition charge and a seat in every course session.

The owner records a payment only after receiving it outside Strap, selecting its installment and a unique receipt reference. Repeating the same reference and facts never records a second payment. Changing those facts is refused. This feature does not collect from a card or bank account.

At each session the owner records attendance. Withdrawal preserves this history. Attendance here issues no makeup credit and grants no guardian or delegated access; the existing class makeup system is separate.

**Preview withdrawal** shows remaining seats and the exact tuition credit before **Confirm withdrawal**. The current conservative policy retains deposits and received tuition, credits only the unpaid part of future installments and leaves overdue tuition owed. This reduces course debt; it does not issue a spendable account credit or send a refund. A payment or attendance change makes the old withdrawal preview unusable. Crossing an installment due time or session start also requires a new preview.

Missed installments show an overdue amount for owner review. They do not trigger automatic collection, messages or withdrawal. The owner explicitly confirms this policy when publishing; its broader product-policy approval remains pending under PLAN-009.

Front desk needs the sale right for enrollment and the separate contract-change right for withdrawal, each scoped to the participant and course location. Both require the existing staff MFA checks and record the request channel. Revocation is checked again even for a repeated request. Only the participant, their payer and authorized staff can read the relevant enrollment; a household contact or guardian receives no course access from that relationship alone.
