# First release

- The owner signs in, manages members and instructors, and publishes a reviewed week of classes; each role sees and changes only its permitted work.
- Members book with approved pack credits; capacity and credits stay consistent, cancellations refund once and promote the first eligible waitlisted member; the assigned instructor records attendance.
- The owner approves pack charges, records externally received payments once, sees balances, and approves prepared reminders. An agent can prepare classes, packs and reminders; only a person approves, with a retained approval record.
- Members acknowledge the exact current waiver version. The owner previews a documented-shape Mindbody member CSV, applies that exact review, reads its journal and undoes untouched rows while preserving later edits.
- The owner exports and restores complete studio data, refuses damaged/populated restores, and erases unused identities with an erasure fence against older backups.
- The run site (`rer build --site`) serves the homepage, whose Run Strap opens `/run`, and the owner guides at `/docs` built from `docs/*.md`; an installed Strap serves the Lobby at `/`.

No card processing, recurring billing, marketplace, video classes, payroll, domain purchase or real provider calls are needed. The CSV fixture is synthetic; no real vendor export compatibility is claimed.
